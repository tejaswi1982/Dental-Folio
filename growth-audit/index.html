See the clinic as a new patient sees it.

A focused review of what makes the practice distinctive, how clearly that value appears online, and what should be improved first.

What it does

More than a website review.

A clinic may offer excellent care and still appear ordinary, unclear or difficult to trust online. The Dental Clinic Growth Audit looks across the patient’s first digital impression, from website and Google profile to social presence, positioning and enquiry journey, to identify where the clinic’s real strengths are being lost.

The outcome is not a long report filled with generic advice. It is a clear view of what is distinct about the clinic, what currently weakens that story, and the few changes most likely to improve trust and response.

The review

What I look at

Defining clinic truth

What patients appear to value most, and what makes the practice meaningfully different.

Website and first impression

Whether the clinic's current website communicates trust, quality and a clear next step.

Google reputation

Rating, review volume, recent review activity, responses and comparison with relevant nearby practices.

Social presence

Whether Instagram or other active channels reinforce the clinic's actual positioning.

Patient journey

How easily a potential patient can understand the clinic, make contact and receive follow-up.

Immediate opportunity

The single most useful direction to prioritise before spending time or money elsewhere.

The outcome

A concise direction, not a hundred-point report.

Each audit is distilled into a short, practical document that identifies:

  • the clinic's defining story;
  • the current digital impression;
  • key trust or clarity gaps;
  • one proposed homepage direction;
  • a small set of priority actions.

Enough to see the opportunity clearly. Not so much that nothing gets done.
